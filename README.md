## The word

**whatchamacallit**

It is the word you reach for when you cannot remember the word — the
only word in the language whose whole job is standing in for a word
you have forgotten. Delete it and the next time the name escapes you
there is nothing left to point at the gap with. You cannot even say
"that whatchamacallit thing" anymore, because the thing you cannot
name is now also the word you cannot say. Every reader has lived
that failure — mid-sentence, hand waving at the air, reaching for
the one word that means "you know, the… that one". Erase it and the
gesture stays but the word is gone, which is exactly why it lands at
first sight: the gap it leaves is the gap it was invented to fill.

`main.ts` validates the word against the gate (one word, 1–77 characters,
not only white space) and writes it, with no trailing newline, to
`/output/entry.txt`.
